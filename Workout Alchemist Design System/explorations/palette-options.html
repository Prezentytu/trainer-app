Palette options — gold reserved for PR only
Same screen fragment, three neutral+one-accent systems. Each keeps exactly one working accent, gold as the PR-only colour, and one red for destructive.

A · Verdigris on warm ink

Deep oxidised green as the single working accent. Warmest, most "apothecary". Green already means done/on-track, so accent and progress share one hue — very calm, slight risk of low contrast between "action" and "completed".

Neutral warm-charcoal surfaces; one desaturated teal as the working accent (buttons, focus, active nav), a lighter mint tint only for completed/positive, gold strictly for PR, terracotta for destructive. Four hues total, none competing — quiet enough for daily 5am use, still unmistakably "yours".

Why C
  • Gold stays scarce. If gold also drives buttons, a PR badge stops feeling special. Teal takes the working load; gold appears maybe twice a week.
  • Two-step green. Saturated teal = "act", lighter mint = "done". Distinct without adding a hue.
  • Neutrals stay neutral. Warm charcoal (not blue-black) keeps skin tones and exercise photos looking right, and lets gold sit on it without clashing.
  • Quiet at 5am. Nothing above ~60% saturation except PR gold; lime (B) glares on a dark gym screen.
  • Trainer + client share it. Teal works as desktop nav/active state; adherence tiers use one hue at three lightnesses instead of a traffic-light rainbow.
Ratio rule
~90% neutral surface + text · ~8% teal · ~2% gold/terracotta. One teal element per screen region; never two primaries in view.
